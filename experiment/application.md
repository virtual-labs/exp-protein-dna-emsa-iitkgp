## Applications

EMSA is a highly sensitive, fast and cost-effective technique.
1. **Detection of DNA-** Protein or RNA-Protein Interactions- to determine if a specific protein binds to a particular DNA or RNA sequence
2. **Characterising Binding specificity-** defining how changes in sequences affect the binding of proteins or nucleic acids
3. **Determination of binding affinity-** EMSA calculates how strongly protein binds to the target sequence
4. **Identifying protein complexes-** EMSA determines whether a protein binding involves cofactors or partner proteins
5. **Screening of inhibitors-** helps in the recognition of molecules disrupting protein-DNA interactions


## Limitations 
1. **Low Resolution-** EMSA defines binding, but provides no clue about the binding site
2. **Difficulty with Complexes-** if multiple protein molecules bind, then gel shifts become very challenging
3. **False Positives and Non-** Specific Binding- Sticky proteins at higher concentrations bind non-specifically to DNA, giving false positive results
4. **Not Suitable for Very Weak Interactions-** If the protein-DNA interaction is very transient or low-affinity, EMSA won't detect it
